# Product principles

These principles guide discovery and trade-offs. Refine them when product
evidence reveals a better rule.

## Evidence before certainty

Separate observations from interpretations and assumptions. Important claims
should link to discovery evidence or an accepted decision.

## Outcomes before features

Describe the change sought for a user before choosing a solution or delivery
mechanism.

## Language is product design

Use the ubiquitous language consistently. If a term is ambiguous, improve the
glossary or identify a context-specific meaning.

## Boundaries should earn their complexity

Model bounded contexts around distinct language, rules, and ownership. Do not
create technical layers and call them domain boundaries.

## Preserve reversibility

Delay expensive, hard-to-reverse choices until evidence makes them necessary.
Record consequential choices and their trade-offs.
